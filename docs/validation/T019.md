## Delivered path and task boundary

The actual workspace/API inspects immutable native CAD for body volume intersections and complete trimmed projected face coverage, outward normal agreement and separation. Detection creates no bond. Candidate/manual selected pairs expose roles, swaps, normal arrows, measured coverage/gap and blockers. Explicit assumptions review is mandatory; physical gaps additionally require a covering SI tolerance and separate approval. Server-native remeasurement, CSRF, ownership/tombstone/head and transactional duplicate-face guards precede persistence.

Saved bond setups append immutable analysis revisions and preserve original geometry/face IDs, source/tool/isolation policy and complete measured reports/hashes. Ordinary edits, historical/current/conflict copies and session undo carry them; geometry changes retain original references but invalidate approval. Actual process restart and browser reload preserve setup/history. Native volume overlap above 1e-12 m³ blocks all multi-body compute admissions. Saved product bonds block solve/export pending **T020** mesh-dependent assembly constraint admission and cannot be silently omitted by the final deck generator. Independent disjoint-body workflows remain covered by regression.

Scope: ≤100 faces, ≤10 saved pairs; complete opposing planar **unit-square faces with four straight native edges**, 1e-8 m numerical coincidence, explicit maximum 1e-5 m physical gap (1e-12 m coordinate-comparison allowance). Candidate diagnosis searches ≤1 mm; this is not approval. Partial/curved-boundary/nonunit/nonopposing/unrelated/excessive pairs refuse. There is no automatic approval, geometry movement/healing, failed native tie fallback, stabilization, remeshing or force redistribution.
