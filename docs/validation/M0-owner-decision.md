# M0 owner decision — proceed with deferred validation

Date: **2026-10-13, Asia/Calcutta**. Decision source: product owner's instruction in the project conversation after [PR #2](https://github.com/shubhs650/OpenFEA/pull/2) merged the M0-R1/M0-R2 fixes. The owner said: “I approve M0, no need for target laptop. My laptop is gaming laptop i5 10 gen (equivalent) + 1650 4 gb graphics. It runs ansys (good enough elements, other sim software) smoothly. We will fully develop this software in codex cloud (at end of M4, we will validate in local laptop), since at last this tool will run in aws as backend.”

**Decision: M0 is approved for progression to M1 under an explicit owner scope exception.** T001–T006 development deliverables may be closed for this milestone; T007 is unblocked and remains NOT STARTED. The earlier [M0 exit review](M0-exit-review.md) is retained as the historical NO-GO at its reviewed commit. Its two reproduced code defects were corrected in PR #2; hosted CI passed on that fix. This owner decision supersedes the earlier stop decision for *milestone progression*, without converting historical development diagnostics into formal benchmark acceptance.

| Item | Decision and boundary |
|---|---|
| F1/F2/F3/F5 | Pinned native toolchain, synthetic face mapping, C3D10 loads and raw stress provenance have real development evidence. Continue building in Codex Cloud. Existing synthetic scope is the supported evidence; arbitrary CAD and new physics still require their scheduled tests. |
| F4 coupling | Continue from the limited zero-gap planar unit-interface mortar proof (at most 65 trace nodes per side) as an experimental development basis. Native `*TIE` failed its frozen patch and remains rejected. Physical gaps, general/scalable assemblies, additional loading and production bond approval are **not** authorized by this decision. Keep physical gaps disabled until T019/B08 and the M3 assembly gate validate them. |
| F6 cleanup | The selected simple hole and straight-edge fillet preview proof is sufficient for M0 progression. General cleanup, apply/undo and face remapping remain T013 work. |
| F7 resource/display | The owner removes target-laptop measurement and headed GPU display as M0 entry requirements. Retained cloud meshes, two real patch solves, stop tests and software-rendered display remain the only measurements; neither a laptop cap nor GPU performance is certified. Keep the existing 30k-element/120k-free-equation diagnostic admission and native resource limits until later measured evidence justifies a change. |
| Formal benchmark acceptance | B01/B02/B07-mortar/B09 manifests still say `PENDING`; the owner did not sign their exact assumptions, samples and criteria hashes here. Development runs stay labelled development-only. Obtain explicit hash-bound engineering-owner review and rerun applicable acceptance cases before claiming accepted benchmarks, at the latest for the M4/T030 local prototype gate; the M3 assembly gate still requires its own reviewed bond evidence. |
| Local and hosted sequence | Implement and test M1–M4 in Codex Cloud using synthetic/public fixtures. At M4/T030 validate the completed end-to-end local workflow, required numerical suite, resource headroom and headed browser on the actual Windows/WSL2 laptop. The owner describes an i5 10th-generation-equivalent gaming laptop and 1650-class GPU with 4 GB VRAM; RAM and exact model remain unverified. Running ANSYS smoothly is encouraging context, not a measured OpenFEA capacity result. |
| AWS | An AWS backend is a later product direction. M5/T033 first requires dated cost, isolation and operational evidence. This decision authorizes no AWS provisioning or deployment, Google authentication, commercial billing or beta operation in the current development stage. |

No frozen physical tolerance, benchmark manifest, native limit or solver output is changed by this decision. Task completion here means the owner accepted the bounded M0 development deliverables and expressly deferred the listed exit evidence; it does not certify a general solver, laptop throughput, accepted benchmarks or readiness for beta.
