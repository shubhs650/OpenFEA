# Local mesh sizing, quality and recovery

Open an analysis pinned to accepted geometry. **Mesh sizing and recovery** saves global size, local body/selected CAD face sizes, curvature refinement and recovery permission in a new immutable analysis revision. Sizes use the current display length unit and convert to canonical metres; source values/units are retained. Unit changes reload saved physical values. Unsaved controls disable mesh submission until saved or reloaded. Meshing can precede material/load setup; solving requires the complete supported setup.

An empty global size uses half each body's largest extent. Local controls refine: the smallest applicable global/body/face size wins, and a face size cannot exceed its resolved body size. Body controls affect that independent body. Face controls use native-mapped CAD surfaces and a distance-based refinement field restricted to the owning volume. Curvature is 0 (off) or 8–64 elements per full circle. Plane/cylinder bodies support actual CAD-projected curved C3D10 mesh inspection; arbitrary surface types and curved load/CCX/result semantics remain unvalidated. Solving retains the affine/planar contract.

**Preview mesh estimate** computes a planning count without saving or starting work. It uses 12 × CAD volume / finest requested size³ over the whole body, including cylindrical curvature. Nodes and DOFs are conservative count estimates, not a memory or runtime guarantee. Native caps remain 3,000 elements, 30,000 nodes, 60 seconds per stage, 180 seconds per job, 1,536 MiB RSS and 192 MiB scratch. Actual generation can exceed a planning count and will stop at the cap.

Oversize settings may be saved for inspection, but job admission refuses them. The visible coarsening proposal changes only the draft when you click its explicitly labelled button: it removes local refinements and turns curvature off. Review the draft and **Save mesh controls** separately before submitting. No automatic coarsening, geometry deletion or tolerance change occurs.

Every generated mesh is checked with the complete quadratic mapping. The Jacobian determinant must be certified above 1e-15 m³ throughout each cell using cubic tetrahedral Bernstein bounds and bounded subdivision; integration-point samples alone cannot establish validity. The dimensionless corner mean ratio must be at least 0.05; values below 0.2 warn. These declared development guards do not establish mesh convergence or design safety. The **Mesh quality overlay** shows all quadratic cell wireframes: green accepted, orange poor-shape warning, red failed. It displays certified minimum determinant and mean ratio. The overlay disables viewport face picking; restore the CAD view for selections. Failed quality blocks solving even when the mesher exits zero.

Recovery is off by default. Saving **I allow two additional disclosed meshing attempts with unchanged sizing** permits at most three attempts: initial Delaunay, Frontal, then Delaunay with Netgen linear optimization before quadratic projection. Exact geometry, sizes, curvature and quality thresholds stay fixed. Only native-generation or quality failures qualify. Mapping, corruption, resource stops, cancellation and interruptions do not retry. Existing per-stage and aggregate job/scratch limits still apply. History shows live attempt/profile and every terminal outcome. `recovery.json`, each `mesh-attempt-N.log` and resource record are downloadable. Failed quality meshes may be inspected as diagnostics; they are not validated results and do not automatically restart.

Queued jobs freeze settings, geometry, setup and tool/source hashes. Subsequent unit/material/load/name edits carry settings forward without altering old artifacts. A physical sizing/curvature or geometry/tool change requires a new compatible mesh before solving. Recovery permission alone does not change connectivity compatibility. Geometry cleanup retains old local references as unresolved blockers until reviewed/removed; undo restores captured controls in a fresh geometry-bound revision. Ownership, tombstones, CSRF and optimistic revision checks apply to reads/writes/publication.
