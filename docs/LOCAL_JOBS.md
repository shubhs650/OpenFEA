# Local mesh and solve jobs

Open a geometry-pinned analysis and review its [saved mesh controls](MESH_CONTROLS.md). **Local mesh and solve** generates actual C3D10 connectivity with global/body/face sizes, curvature and complete quadratic quality guards, at most 3,000 elements and 60 seconds per native stage. Meshing can precede materials/loads; solving requires confirmed per-body materials, saved supported loads/driving displacement and sufficient global component/fixed constraints on each independent body. Curved plane/cylinder meshes are preview-only; solve/export retains the validated affine/planar contract. Explicit recovery permission allows at most two additional native profiles with unchanged sizing and retained diagnostics. No automatic healing, stabilizing, bonding or coarsening. T029 permits one identified infrastructure retry after fencing, within the original job budget.

1. Click **Generate local mesh**. A durable mesh job freezes the current analysis and builds actual Gmsh connectivity in isolation. Stage status and heartbeat come from the server.
2. Choose a completed **Checked mesh** and click **Preview selected mesh**. The viewport shows its actual quadratic boundary triangles and node/element counts; catalog face picking and load arrows remain available. **Show CAD geometry** restores the original display. Changing the checked mesh clears the old preview.
3. Inspect the optional all-cell **Mesh quality overlay**, then review the selected compatible mesh and current small-deformation linear elastic inputs, check the review box and click **Solve selected mesh**. The server freezes the current revision, checked mesh and regenerated CalculiX deck before queueing. Material/load edits can reuse geometry/connectivity with a new frozen deck; physical sizing/curvature or geometry/tool changes require a new mesh. Failed quality cannot be solved.

One heavy operation runs at a time, including STEP inspection and synchronous deck exports. At most 20 pending/active jobs are admitted. You can continue editing while work is queued or running. Closing/reloading the browser does not cancel it. History records the original analysis revision, geometry and input SHA-256 even if the editor has moved on. A lost submission response can be retried with unchanged inputs and the same request key without duplicating execution.

**Cancel** immediately stops a pending job. A running job first shows **cancelling**, then **cancelled** after confirmed process termination. Partial files remain diagnostic; they are not valid results. Project deletion hides access immediately and requests cancellation. API restart resumes pending work; a previously running attempt is fenced and recorded **interrupted**, then can consume the one infrastructure retry within the original 180-second deadline. History keeps both execution records. A completed verified CCX checkpoint is reused for validation only; corrupt data or exhausted budget fails without another solve. Reopening the local session restores access to saved history and downloads. If the worker cannot fence previous execution, it blocks rather than starting more work.

Successful mesh jobs provide checked mesh/snapshot/log/resource/recovery downloads and a deck when the frozen affine setup is complete. History shows attempt/profile and fixed-name per-attempt logs/resources. Failed quality meshes may be inspected as diagnostics. Successful solve jobs additionally provide raw `analysis.dat`, `analysis.frd`, `solver.log` and canonical `results.json`. The latter retains SI displacement, external force vectors and complete global integration-point stress tensors with body/material/source provenance. Native FRD extrapolated/averaged stresses are explicitly labelled diagnostics, since they may cross discontinuities. Frozen setup and native warnings appear in history and parsed outputs. [T012 core results](CORE_RESULTS.md) supplies calculated views, deformation, extrema and yield factors; none establishes convergence or design safety.

Exit code zero alone does not mean success. Missing finish markers, no actual free equations, recognized numerical errors/singularity, bad/missing/truncated/nonfinite outputs, incompatible ownership/topology/frame, resource limits or missing verified artifacts fail the job. A fully constrained model with no free equations is currently refused. Failed outputs can be downloaded for private diagnostics; `results.json` is available only after validated successful completion. Generated decks are downloadable and inspectable; raw user-authored decks are never accepted for execution.

Artifacts and frozen inputs are hash-verified, owner-scoped and retained across edits/restarts. Native jobs cannot access project storage outside their readonly inputs/private scratch or use network sockets. See [ADR-019](adr/ADR-019.md), [API contract](API_CONTRACT.md) and [executed evidence](validation/T011.md). Windows/Edge, target laptop/headed GPU, larger meshes/assemblies, global retention/purge, signed benchmarks and complete M1/M4 acceptance remain scheduled gates.

Native scratch peaks retain both live samples and the final audit after child exit, so a short-lived writer cannot leave a stale low peak in retained diagnostics. The original aggregate native budget/refusal remains unchanged; T029 adds bounded infrastructure recovery under [ADR-035](adr/ADR-035.md); [T015 evidence](validation/T015.md) includes a deterministic actual native probe regression.

The job history shows **Execution N of 2**, the original deadline and whether verified solver outputs were reused. Each completed execution has owned diagnostic downloads. Result parsing runs in its own supervised, networkless sandbox while the controller continues cancellation and heartbeats. The logical deadline includes validation, restart downtime and retry queue waits. Disk reserve is checked during work; scratch from both executions shares 192 MiB, logs are capped at 8 MiB, and unique retained artifacts share 64 MiB. Resource/model failures and unknown parser exits do not automatically retry. Sampling can overshoot; this is not a laptop capacity guarantee. See [T029 evidence](validation/T029.md).
