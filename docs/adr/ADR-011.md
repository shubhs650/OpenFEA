# ADR-011: Revision-bound synthetic face mapping and affine C3D10 export

Status: Accepted for development scope; engineering benchmark acceptance pending
Date: 2026-10-12

## Decision

T003 uses separate OCCT and Gmsh processes with version-1 SI file contracts. Allowlisted synthetic cube, planar L-bracket and two separated cubes are written/read as explicitly metre-based STEP, validated by OCCT, then frozen as canonical BREP and per-body BREP files. The SHA-256 of canonical BREP is the geometry revision. Body and face IDs include that revision and remain attached to display triangles, meshed boundary faces and generated solver surfaces. Transient Gmsh tags are never the persisted selection identity.

Match planar faces within their imported body by area, centroid, bounds and adjacency. Require a one-to-one mapping before physical groups or loads. Freeze area comparison at 1e-8 m² and coordinate comparison at 1e-6 m (Gmsh bounds include kernel padding). Missing, ambiguous, duplicate or stale identity is explicitly unresolved. These criteria have evidence for the synthetic fixtures only; arbitrary STEP, remapping after edits and general face confidence remain T013 work. Bodies are imported/meshed separately without fusing.

Gmsh tetra10 connectivity maps to CCX C3D10 with zero-based permutation `(0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,9,8)`. Every midside must match its intended edge midpoint within 1e-9 m. Corner determinant minus a conservative quadratic-map determinant perturbation bound must exceed 1e-15 m³ everywhere in the reference tetrahedron. This bounds derivatives of midpoint deviations over the full domain; it is stronger than corner-volume or integration-point-only sampling. Curved/non-midpoint volume elements are explicitly rejected in this development slice. No node coordinates are changed to pass quality checks.

Use oriented six-node exterior faces and degree-five seven-point Dunavant quadrature for consistent nodal forces. Positive pressure is inward. Distributed force is one total vector divided by the entire deduplicated selected area, including shared nodes. Tests prove all four face orientations, curved polynomial pressure resultants/moments, equal/unequal area selections and pressure reversal. Arbitrary curved-surface area/traction accuracy is not established; the end-to-end exporter remains planar/affine only.

Decks contain deterministic C3D10 topology, face-reference comments, named element surfaces, material, explicit constraints and generated loads. Numeric fields use 12 decimal places in scientific notation to fit CCX's input field width. No nonzero nodal load is dropped. No public uploaded-deck execution is added.

## Evidence and limits

[T003 evidence](../validation/T003.md) records the real file/process/deck/solver path, tested commit and pinned versions. CCX `RF` is reaction plus applied loads, as documented in the pinned 2.23 manual's *NODE PRINT section; subtract known CLOAD values before reporting reactions. The single-step diagnostic DAT reader is not the T004 production result parser and does not establish smoothed/unaveraged contour semantics.

The owner explicitly chose **development-only; review later** for B01/B02 manifests on 2026-10-12. Their frozen proposed criteria pass diagnostically; signed release acceptance and formal T003 closure remain open. ADR-003's tie proposal is unaffected. No new dependency, deployment, UI workflow or complete hostile-input sandbox is introduced.
