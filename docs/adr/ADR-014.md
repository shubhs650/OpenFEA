# ADR-014 — Bounded immutable cleanup and resource diagnostics

Status: DEVELOPMENT DECISION, 2026-10-13 Asia/Calcutta. Engineering/laptop acceptance pending.

T006 uses native OCCT `BRepAlgoAPI_Defeaturing` on a deep geometry/topology copy, selected by source BREP revision/body/face reference. Only the two synthetic unit-solid fixtures are supported: radius 0.1 m axial through-hole and one straight-edge fillet. Output must remain one valid solid, with six planar faces, unit volume and the independent analytical feature-volume delta within 1e-10 m³. Source bytes are verified unchanged on success and failure. Preview is a separate revision with tessellation; old selections/mesh/results are invalidated. General healing, imported curved CAD, persistent apply/undo and remapping remain T013. No reconstruction of the cube substitutes for the native removal operation.

Graduated Gmsh→C3D10→CCX workloads follow the predeclared [T006 manifest](../../tests/benchmarks/T006.json), retaining actual counts rather than promising exact target meshes. Physics/sampling/tolerances reuse frozen B01 without widening. A conservative admission ceiling is 30k elements and 120k free equations for this patch, with separate 1.5 GiB RSS, 256 MiB scratch and 90 s wall budgets. This is a development guard, not a general sparse-solver estimate or certified laptop cap. No automatic coarsening/retry or native-limit relaxation occurs. Native 60 s CPU/4 GiB AS/64 MiB file/socket limits still apply.

Kernel child high-water RSS and CPU are recorded by a known isolated Python wrapper; a 50 ms owned-process-tree RSS sum and directory-size watchdog stops runaway jobs. Snapshot shared pages may be double-counted, short peaks may be missed and disk/RSS stops can overshoot by one sample. Kernel maxRSS excludes the Python wrapper; sampled tree RSS includes it. Killed groups may lack final kernel counters; that is recorded, never fabricated. This is trusted synthetic workload isolation, not hostile-upload security. Orchestration reads a compact mesh summary instead of loading an unbounded mesh in the parent. Admission guard runs again in the bounded preparation stage.

The standalone WebGL diagnostic draws actual exterior mesh triangles and actual DAT fields. Raw IP1 xx is piecewise constant per exterior element and labelled explicitly, without averaging; it is not a general extrapolated stress viewer. Rendering performance covers exterior surfaces only. Target-machine headed execution must classify the renderer; headless SwiftShader is development evidence only. Cloud hardware cannot certify a 16 GiB WSL2 laptop or integrated GPU.

M0 remains open while owner reviews and target-hardware evidence are pending. Native *TIE remains rejected; experimental mortar remains limited to its predeclared planar zero-gap/65-trace-node scope. No product-approved scope exception or physical-gap acceptance is inferred from a request to implement T006.
