# ADR-003: C3D10 and nonconforming ties

Status: Proposed
Date: 2026-10-12

## Decision
C3D10 meshes per body and validated CCX tied interfaces.

## Rationale
Matches chosen solids/assemblies scope without hiding bonds by fusing geometry.

## Evidence/gate
F3/F4 establish permutations, coverage, gap and adjustment behavior.

## Alternatives not selected
Automatic contact, node merging as a universal bond substitute

## Consequences
Maintain the boundary in implementation and tests. If evidence contradicts this choice, add a superseding ADR with affected tasks; do not overwrite historical rationale. No executed validation is asserted by this record.

## T005 development evidence — 2026-10-13

[T005](../validation/T005.md) found that native node-sampled *TIE fails the declared nonmatching quadratic axial patch. [ADR-013](ADR-013.md) records a separately declared consistent mortar *EQUATION experiment passing unchanged physical criteria in both orientations. Its planar unit/65-trace-node scope, dense implementation and pending engineering review do not validate general bonds or physical gaps. The original native candidate remains failed, not an accepted fallback.
