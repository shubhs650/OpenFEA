# ADR-022 — Revisioned sizing, certified quadratic mesh quality and controlled recovery

Status: Accepted for bounded development implementation; criteria declared before execution, [executed T014 evidence](../validation/T014.md) retained. Engineering-owner benchmark/laptop acceptance remains deferred.
Date: 2026-10-14 (Asia/Calcutta)

T014 stores canonical SI global/body/face sizes per immutable analysis revision. The finest applicable size wins; local face controls refine their body. Curvature sizing is 0 (off) or 8–64 elements per full circle. Native CAD face/body references, settings and source units are frozen before admission. Different geometry or physical sizing invalidates mesh reuse. Geometry changes retain local controls as unresolved until explicit review/removal; undo restores captured settings. No silent coarsening.

Use actual Gmsh tetra10 with the existing CCX permutation and oriented tri6 connectivity. Planar bodies retain affine midsides. Plane/cylinder bodies may produce actual CAD-projected quadratic midsides for mesh inspection. The existing affine/planar load/CCX/result contract remains enforced until curved load/solve validation; a valid curved preview alone cannot authorize solving.

Before execution, freeze the following development quality criteria: determinant everywhere >1e-15 m³ (existing ADR-011 floor), dimensionless corner mean ratio >=0.05, warning below 0.2. Certify the cubic determinant of the affine-in-reference-coordinate C3D10 Jacobian using tetrahedral Bernstein coefficients, outward floating-point margin and at most three centroid subdivisions. Sampling at corners or integration points alone is insufficient. Uncertified/negative/degenerate cells fail quality and block solve. The new shape-quality floor is a disclosed development guard, not an engineering-owner benchmark acceptance or a replacement for convergence checks. Never change coordinates or thresholds to obtain quality acceptance.

Recovery is optional, explicitly approved in saved setup, within the original logical/native/RSS/scratch budgets: initial Delaunay (algorithm 1), at most one Frontal (algorithm 4) alternative, then one Delaunay with Netgen linear optimization before quadratic projection. All profiles retain exact sizing, curvature controls, geometry and quality criteria. Preserve every attempt's profile/settings/outcome/log/resources. Only native generation or quality failures qualify; mapping, resource, corruption, cancellation and interruptions do not trigger recovery. Failed jobs do not restart. At most three native mesh attempts, no solver retry. The declared alternatives require actual native execution evidence before completion.

A conservative count estimate uses 12×CAD volume/minimum requested size³, extending the minimum for cylindrical curvature. It is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed bound or universal bytes-per-element memory model. Native 3,000-element/30,000-node, 60-second/1,536-MiB/192-MiB-stage and 180-second-job guards remain authoritative. Oversize settings/models produce a visible coarsening proposal requiring explicit save/approval; no automatic size changes. The 100k target and actual laptop measurements remain M4 obligations.

The real browser must save/display settings, inspect actual quadratic meshes/quality and diagnostics, disclose recovery progress and histories, retain old frozen runs, and enforce all blockers server-side. Synthetic analytical Jacobian references, real local override/curvature effects, native retry exhaustion and cap/coarsening, immutable/restart/owner checks and Chromium evidence are required. T014 adds no hosted pricing, private CAD, deployment or later analysis types.
