# ADR-013: Experimental consistent mortar coupling for nonmatching C3D10

Status: Accepted for development experiment; engineering-owner acceptance pending
Date: 2026-10-13 (Asia/Calcutta)

## Problem and measured decision

The pinned CCX node-to-face `*TIE,NAME=BOND,POSITION TOLERANCE=1e-8,ADJUST=NO` connects all 65 intended fine-side nodes without changing coordinates, but fails the frozen B07 axial patch: maximum stress error 183,365 Pa against 1 MPa and unacceptable near-zero displacement components. Satisfying sampled nodal constraints alone does not establish consistent interface virtual work on nonmatching quadratic meshes. This rejected candidate and its original manifest remain retained; no tolerance or exclusion was changed to make it pass.

Use a separately declared, experimental planar mortar projection for this M0 proof: `M_slave P = C_slave,master`, with trace mass and cross-mass integrated over intersections of the two affine quadratic boundary triangulations. Degree-five Dunavant quadrature integrates the degree-four basis products exactly; triangular intersection polygons are integrated by a fan subdivision. Solve the positive-definite slave mass by Cholesky without regularization, stabilization or added stiffness. Check affine reproduction, full area and uniform-traction virtual-work transfer before exporting coefficients.

Emit three homogeneous `*EQUATION` constraints per slave node, with the slave translation first (coefficient 1) and independent master translations following (coefficients `-P`). CCX performs the actual structural solve. This is an interface adapter, not a replacement finite-element solver. The pinned *EQUATION manual documents dependent-variable elimination and prohibits duplicate dependent SPC/MPC DOFs. Keep every nonzero coefficient, source mesh/body/face identity, and deterministic finite-precision deck.

## Scope and safeguards

The CLI proof is two separately imported, independently sized SI blocks with disjoint node IDs, C3D10 throughout and the same physical model/reference/sampling as B07 revision 1. Revision 2 changes only the interface formulation; its proposed 2% displacement, 5% stress, 0.5% equilibrium and absolute criteria remain identical. Both master/slave orientations pass. The monolithic reference is generated independently; bodies in the bonded case are never fused or node-merged.

Only complete opposing planar zero-gap unit interfaces with **at most 65 trace nodes per side** are supported by this experimental path. Dense projection is not a scalable assembly implementation. Reject incomplete/stale selections, shared node IDs, overlap, physical gaps, inconsistent face membership, nonplanarity and slave SPC/MPC conflicts. Body-to-support graph checks reject detached components but do not prove stiffness rank. No automatic bond approval or mesh-size change is introduced.

CCX *TIE adjusts coordinates by default. A controlled 10 µm gap diagnostic measured approximately 10 µm adjustment; `ADJUST=NO` measured zero. It is **not** approved gap physics or B08 acceptance. The supported coupling rejects physical gaps, and `*EQUATION` changes no coordinates. A narrow pinned little-endian binary-FRD geometry audit reads native doubles and compares with emitted deck coordinates, avoiding false zero-motion claims from rounded ASCII output. T004's general binary stress parsing remains unsupported.

Native partial-tie coverage warnings are fatal to credited solves. The untied assembly returned exit 0/“Job finished” with ~9.79e9 m displacement; the supported path rejects its detached body before launching. Do not infer solver validity from its exit status. External reactions include prescribed DOFs only; internal MPC forces are not counted as supports.

## Evidence and consequences

[T005 evidence](../validation/T005.md) records versions, commit, all samples, exact keywords, rejected candidates and compressed raw artifacts. Mortar is a **development alternative** to ADR-003's proposed native tie choice. The owner must review the formulation and B07 manifest before formal F4/T005 acceptance. Larger/general faces, assemblies, gaps, multi-bond dependencies, other load cases and scalability require later validation (T006/T019/M3). The native *TIE candidate is not enabled as a validated feature. No solver source patch, fake result, tolerance widening, deployment or merge is used.
